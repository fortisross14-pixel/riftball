# RIFTBALL game interface (all screens)

Scope: the whole game frontend (Chronicle, Tourneys, Bestiary, Armorial, Lists/match, Rolls, Annals, Colophon). Mode: Experience with Operate-grade data screens. Audience: a single spectator-chronicler on desktop or landscape tablet. Job: advance time, follow stories, watch sealed replays, dig into numbers. Constraints: engine, saves and spoiler sealing unchanged; offline; comedic voice; vector art with a raster drop-in registry.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The game is one great illuminated codex lying open on a scriptorium desk; every section is a spread of that same book and time advances by turning its page. It refuses the dark sidebar dashboard of framed panels and card grids.

OWN-WORLD: Vellum pages with deckled warmth and foxing, set on dark oak under candlelight; oxblood leather binding with brass corner bosses; lapis, vermilion, verdigris, murex purple and burnished gold leaf as pigment fields that own whole regions (historiated initials, miniature grounds, rubric bands, ribbons). Ink is iron-gall brown-black; rubrics red. Blackletter display (Grenze Gotisch), calligraphic humanist text (Alegreya / Alegreya SC / Alegreya Sans for ledgers). Gold leaf is reserved for now/next and honours.

STORY: The reader opens the book on this week's Chronicle, turns to any section by its silk ribbon, opens a folio for any athlete, house or contest, breaks a wax seal to learn a result, and turns the page to write the next week.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The open book fills the desk with a visible gutter; seven ribbons hang from the headband at top right of the spine; running head carries Year and Week; left page: lead story under a large gold historiated initial with a miniature of the featured contest; right page: Tidings column with marginal drolleries, Order of Precedence, and the page corner "Turn the page" control bottom right.

FORM: Illuminated manuscript codex, position 1 on my ordered list (chosen as IMPECCABLE'S PICK over the dealt Tapestry Hall). seed:b1d52cdf30bcbdca. Signature interaction: the page-turn (navigation and advancing time both turn a leaf); motion grammar: leaf turns, ink writing-on for new text, gold glint on honours, wax seal crack for reveals.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
