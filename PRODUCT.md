# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

A single player acting as the world's chronicler and spectator. They found a fantasy sports universe, advance time (one week to ten years), watch recorded matches unfold, and read the history that the simulation writes. They control no club. Sessions are leisure play on desktop or a landscape tablet, with mouse or touch.

## Product Purpose

RIFTBALL: World Chronicle is an offline, seeded fantasy sports world simulator. Success is the player wanting to advance "one more week" because the world produces stories, rivalries, upsets and legends worth following, and being able to dig into every number behind them.

## Positioning

The simulation writes its own memory: storylines (droughts, dynasties, rivalries, records, prodigies, last dances, upsets, heartbreaks) persist across seasons and later stories refer back to them. Every match has a deterministic replay, box score and drivebook. It is a living sporting history, not a management sim.

## Operating Context

- World: 80 clubs, 4 regions (Iron Marches / The Foundries, Verdant Realms / The Crownlands, Ashen Coast / The Free Ports, Gloamlands / The Shadow Territories), Premier and Challenger divisions of 10 each.
- 1,200 active athletes; 9 races (Human, Orc, Elf, Dwarf, Goblin, Troll, Lizardkin, Revenant, Satyr); 6 roles (Bastion, Breaker, Runner, Thrower, Raider, Trickster); 6 rarities (Ordinary to Generational); 12 attributes; traits, Spark abilities, levels, injuries, ageing, retirement.
- Season of 26 weeks: 18 league rounds home and away, regional semis (19) and finals (20), Grand Rift quarterfinals (21), semis (22), World Final (23), awards (24), offseason (25–26). Promotion and relegation.
- Scoring vocabulary: breach, gate, aftershock, interception, knockout, fumble, big play, spark, penalty, injury.
- Club philosophies: Iron Wall, Silver Arrow, Stampede, Mischief, Clockwork, Swarm.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- React 19 + TypeScript + Vite; deployed to GitHub Pages with `base: './'`.
- Engine (`src/engine.ts`, `src/chronicle.ts`) and types are authoritative and must not change behaviour; engine tests in `src/engine.test.ts`.
- Persistence: IndexedDB with revision checks, cross-tab conflict detection via BroadcastChannel, JSON export/import (`riftball-world-v1`/`v2`). Existing saves must keep loading.
- Spoiler-safe replays: a completed match's result stays sealed until the replay reaches the end or the player reveals it.
- Legacy matches without a `report` show only their highlight ledger; never fabricate their missing numbers.
- Must work fully offline: no external image services; artwork ships locally.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: RIFTBALL — World Chronicle. The world championship is "The Grand Rift".
- Voice: comedic. Keep the tongue-in-cheek humour throughout (referees needing insurance, the medical guild issuing warnings).
- Artwork: custom vector art built in code for now; the asset system must be structured so painted raster portraits and crests supplied later can replace it per race/club without code changes elsewhere.

## Evidence on Hand

All content is generated by the simulation: club names, athlete names, stories, results, records. No real-world testimonials, press or licensed artwork exist; none may be invented.

## Product Principles

1. The world is the protagonist: every screen should surface stories and people, not just tables.
2. Depth on demand: dense statistics are always one tap away, never forced up front.
3. Never spoil: results are revealed by the player's choice.
4. The save is sacred: no UI change may risk or silently break a stored universe.
